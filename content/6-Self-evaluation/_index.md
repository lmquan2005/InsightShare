During my internship at **Amazon Web Services Vietnam Co., Ltd.** (the **Workforce Bootcamp – First Cloud AI Journey** program, group **AWS062026**) from **15 June 2026** to **7 August 2026**, I had the opportunity to learn, practice and apply the knowledge acquired at **Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (HCMUT)** in a real cloud environment.

The focus of the internship was building and deploying **InsightShare** — a serverless image and document sharing platform on AWS, integrated with **Amazon Rekognition**, **Amazon Textract** and **Amazon Bedrock (Claude)** to label images, extract text and answer document questions in Vietnamese. Through this project I implemented end-to-end **S3**, **Lambda (Python)**, **API Gateway**, **DynamoDB**, **CloudFront**, **CloudWatch** and **IAM**; and completed the Proposal, 8-week worklog, hands-on workshop and bilingual EN/VI report.

In terms of work ethic, I strived to complete tasks on schedule, followed FCAJ rules, proactively documented issues encountered (IAM `AccessDeniedException`, DynamoDB `Decimal` serialization, Bedrock quota limits, etc.) and discussed solutions with mentors and group members.

### Achievements

* Understood the serverless flow from static frontend → API Gateway → Lambda → S3/DynamoDB, with an AI layer on top.
* Independently deployed and verified upload, AI analysis, content-based search and document Q&A on real AWS infrastructure.
* Configured IAM with least-privilege principles and monitored Lambda through CloudWatch.
* Completed a bilingual internship report, Proposal and workshop documenting the full deployment process.

### Needs Improvement

* **Time discipline:** plan earlier for the final weeks (report wrap-up, resource cleanup) to avoid cramming tasks.
* **Operations mindset:** go deeper into monitoring, alerting and incident handling in production environments, not only at demo scale.
* **Technical communication:** practice presenting architecture and product demos to larger audiences, including in English.
* **GenAI on AWS:** learn more about Bedrock quotas, cost-effective model selection and RAG patterns when scaling InsightShare.

### Conclusion

The FCAJ internship helped me move from basic AWS knowledge (IAM, EC2, VPC, S3) to deploying a real serverless application with AI integration. I am more confident reading AWS documentation, debugging permission errors and building a complete solution from idea to a working product. This is a solid foundation to continue toward **Cloud Engineer** and **AI on AWS** after the program ends.
