During the **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** internship at **AWS Vietnam** (group **AWS062026**, 15/06 – 07/08/2026), I built the **InsightShare** capstone — a serverless document assistant on AWS with Rekognition, Textract and Bedrock. Below is my honest feedback to help the FCAJ team improve future cohorts.

### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The office on the 26th floor of Bitexco Financial Tower is quiet and well-equipped, which made it easy to focus during lab sessions and community events. Coming in on a fixed schedule helped me build a steadier work habit than studying alone at home. The AWS branding and event space also made presentations and meetups feel professional without being intimidating.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  
Mentor **Nguyen Gia Hung** and the admin team stayed close throughout the internship. When I hit **IAM `AccessDeniedException`** errors or **CORS** issues while moving InsightShare to Lambda + API Gateway, they pointed me toward the right approach instead of fixing it for me — so the root cause was clearer after I solved it myself. The admin team also helped with Portal registration, attendance and report requirements on time.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  
The InsightShare project fits **Computer Science** well: it used programming, networking and database fundamentals from university, and extended into **cloud and serverless architecture** — areas the curriculum does not cover in depth. Building a real end-to-end product felt more meaningful than isolated homework assignments.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
I gained hands-on experience with **S3, Lambda (Python/boto3), API Gateway, DynamoDB, CloudFront, IAM, CloudWatch**, plus **Rekognition, Textract and Bedrock**. I also practiced writing bilingual technical docs (worklog, workshop, blogs) and deploying a Hugo report site to GitHub Pages. The three FCAJ events (Saturday Meetup, Community Day, study tour) added perspectives on AI products and career direction.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
Knowledge is shared openly during meetups and in the cohort group — asking questions felt natural, not embarrassing. Seeing peers present capstone projects (e.g. IoT + multi-agent designs) motivated me to polish my own architecture diagram and demo.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**  
The program provides free learning and hands-on practice on AWS resources, a structured lab roadmap, community events and support for the internship stamp. One friction point: onboarding info about attendance, seals and report format is spread across several pages — consolidating it into a single checklist would shorten the first week.

---

### Additional Questions

**What did you find most satisfying?**  
Seeing InsightShare run end-to-end on AWS — upload a file, get AI labels and text, search by content, ask questions in Vietnamese via Bedrock — after weeks of debugging IAM and DynamoDB serialization.

**What should be improved for future interns?**  
A short **sample capstone timeline** (week-by-week milestones) and one **office hour** focused on common pitfalls (IAM least-privilege, presigned URLs, Bedrock quotas) would save time in weeks 5–8.

**Would you recommend this program to a friend?**  
Yes. FCAJ combines structured AWS learning, a real capstone project, community events and direct exposure to AWS Vietnam — valuable for anyone aiming at cloud or AI engineering roles.

---

### Suggestions & Expectations

- Add periodic sessions showcasing **sample capstone topics** so interns can choose a direction earlier.
- Keep the **Community Day** and **study tour** formats — they bridge technical learning and industry reality.
- Maintain and expand the program for future cohorts; I would gladly stay connected with the FCAJ community after graduation.
